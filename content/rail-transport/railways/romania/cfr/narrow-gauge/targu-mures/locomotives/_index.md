### Motive Power

Motive power on the Târgu Mureș system was originally provided by **twelve former MÁV 0-8-0T tank locomotives**. In later years these were supplemented by a variety of CFR machines. The majority were standard Romanian 0-8-0Ts, together with some locomotives acquired from elsewhere.

Among the non-standard locomotives was a **Saxon Class IV K, no. 148**, a Günther-Meyer articulated design of Saxon origin that remained in service at Târgu Mureș until its withdrawal.

Two **Px48 0-8-0 tender locomotives supplied from Poland in 1948** also operated on the system. These powerful tender locomotives proved very successful, and as a result the **Malaxa Works** (later known as **Uzinele 23 August / U23A**) produced **ten slightly heavier variants of this design in 1949**, all of which operated on the Târgu Mureș system.

At the time of writing, **0-8-0 no. 764.053** (Polish tender) and **0-8-0T no. 764.159** (Malaxa tank) were retained at Târgu Mureș in connection with the proposed tourist operation.
