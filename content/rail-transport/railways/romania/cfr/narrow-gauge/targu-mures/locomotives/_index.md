### Motive Power

Motive power was originally provided by twelve former MÁV 0-8-0T tank locomotives, which were followed in later years by a variety of CFR machines. The majority of these were Romanian built 0-8-0 and 0-8-0T, together with some locomotives acquired from elsewhere, including the Saxon Class IV K no. 148 Günther-Meyer articulated locomotive left behind by the Austro-Hungarian troops after WWI, and two Px48 0-8-0 tender locomotives supplied from Poland in 1948.

The powerful Polish locomotives proved very successful; as a result, the Malaxa Works (founded in 1921) — from 1948 known as "Uzinele 23 August" and, from 1990, "Faur" — produced ten slightly heavier variants of the same design in 1949, all of which operated on the Târgu Mureș system.

Ten other locomotives built by "Uzinele 23 August" in 1949-50 for the Târgu Mureș system were based on the 1923 Schwartzkopff 0-8-0T design, numbered 764.150 to 764.159. Chunky and compact, these were the heaviest of the network's tank engines and became closely associated with Târgu Mureș.

1.23. During the final decade of the Targu Mures system, "Uzinele 23 August" 0-8-0T no. 764.159 and Chrzanow 0-8-0 no. 764.053 double head a passenger train shortly after departing from Targu Mures, en route to Milhesu de Campie, on 29th June 1995. (D.Trevor Rowe). A pairing that neatly illustrates the network's two-batch fleet.

1.24. Two L45H diesel locomotives, nos 87 0015 and 87 0008, were recorded at Targu Mures depot on 29th June 1995. The L45H was built by "Uzinele 23 August" between 1967 and 1985. A total of 334 locomotives of this type were built. (D.Trevor Rowe)
