### Motive Power

Motive power was originally provided by **twelve former MÁV 0-8-0T tank locomotives**, which were followed in later years by a variety of CFR machines. The majority of these were **standard Romanian 0-8-0Ts**, together with some locomotives acquired from elsewhere.

These included a **Saxon Class IV K Günther-Meyer articulated locomotive, no. 148**, and **two Px48 0-8-0 tender locomotives supplied from Poland** in 1948. These powerful tender locomotives proved very successful; as a result, the Malaxa Works — later known as Uzinele 23 August (U23A) — produced **ten slightly heavier variants of the same design** in 1949, all of which operated on the Târgu Mureș system.

The U23A locomotives were based on the 1923 Schwartzkopff 0-8-0T design. Schwartzkopff no. **764.004**, built in 1923, was still working on the system in September 1966, when it was photographed at Praid, Eremitu and at a water stop while working a train to Târgu Mureș. It was fitted with an auxiliary tender, apparently a later addition.

U23A 0-8-0T no. **764.157**, built in 1949, was photographed at Band and at intermediate stops while working a passenger train from Târgu Mureș to Miheșu de Câmpie on 17 September 1966. Its resemblance to the 1923 Schwartzkopff design is evident in photographs of the two classes.

In the system’s final decade, U23A 0-8-0T no. **764.159** and Chrzanów-built 0-8-0 no. **764.053** were photographed double-heading a passenger train shortly after leaving Târgu Mureș for Miheșu de Câmpie on 29 June 1995. No. 764.159 was later restored and recorded hauling a special train from Târgu Mureș towards Miheșu de Câmpie on 8 May 1996.

Diesel traction was also used on the system. Two **L45H B-B diesel locomotives**, nos **87-0015** and **87-0008**, were recorded at Târgu Mureș depot on 29 June 1995. The L45H was built by U23A between 1964 and 1983, with 48 locomotives completed.

0-8-0 no. **764.052** works on the Sovata–Câmpu Cetății heritage narrow-gauge railway.
